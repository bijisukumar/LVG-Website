Welcome to Dwarka

Your Home Away from Home in Guruvayur

Dwarka is more than just a holiday rental — it's a sanctuary for families visiting the sacred Guruvayur Sri Krishna Temple. Nestled in a peaceful residential neighbourhood, our beautifully maintained villa offers the perfect blend of traditional Kerala hospitality and modern comfort.

Unlike impersonal hotel rooms, we provide a genuine home experience where your family can gather, cook traditional meals, and rest peacefully after your temple visits.
