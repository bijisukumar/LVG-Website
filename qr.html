UTM Tracking Links & QR Codes

Each channel gets a unique link. When a guest books, you'll see exactly where they came from — WhatsApp, Instagram, temple poster, or anywhere else.

All links go to: luxuryvillasofguruvayur.com/book.html?src=[channel]  —  guests land on the booking form, you see the source.

How to Track Performance

📊 Google Analytics (Free)

Add GA4 to your site and every ?src= visit is tracked automatically. See which channel brings the most visitors.

💬 WhatsApp Messages

When a guest submits the form, the WhatsApp message you receive will include their source: "Source: wa1"

📧 Email Subject Line

Every booking request email includes the source in the body — so you know which channel drove each enquiry.

🔗 Bitly (Optional)

Paste any link into bitly.com to get click counts. Free plan shows total clicks per link per day.
