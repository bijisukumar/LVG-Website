Frequently Asked Questions

How far is the villa from Guruvayur Temple?
Just 5 minutes by car or auto-rickshaw. We're located near the West Nada entrance, making it very convenient for multiple temple visits throughout the day.
Do you provide airport pickup?
Yes, we arrange pickups from Cochin International Airport (75 km), Calicut Airport, and Thrissur Railway Station. Please let us know your travel details when booking.
Is the villa suitable for senior citizens?
Absolutely. We have ground-floor bedroom options, grab bars in bathrooms, and our caretaker provides assistance. We're also close to medical facilities in Guruvayur town.
Can we cook our own meals?
Yes! The villa has a fully equipped modular kitchen with all appliances and cookware. We can also arrange a local cook who specialises in traditional Kerala vegetarian cuisine.
What is the check-in and check-out time?
Standard check-in is 2 PM and check-out is 11 AM. We're flexible for early check-in or late check-out based on availability — just let us know your travel schedule.
