Kerala is the birthplace of Ayurveda. We connect you with certified physicians and therapists for personalised Panchakarma treatments, herbal massages, and rejuvenating therapies — at your villa or curated centres nearby.

Hatha YogaMeditationPranayama
Wildlife & Nature
Wildlife Experience

From the majestic elephants of Punnathur Kotta — the world's largest captive elephant sanctuary — to the biodiversity of Anamalai. We arrange guided safaris, elephant interactions, and birdwatching tours for your family.

Elephant SanctuaryJungle SafariBirdwatchingWildlife Photography
Farm Experience

Connect with Kerala's agricultural roots — pick coconuts, harvest rice, press coconut oil, or walk through paddy fields at golden hour. Ideal for children wanting to experience the authentic rhythms of rural Kerala life.

Coconut Farm WalkRice HarvestTraditional CookingKids Activity

Thrissur is the cultural capital of Kerala — and Dwarka places you at its heart. Witness Kathakali performances, attend Thrissur Pooram, or arrange an Arangettam (dance debut) for your child at the villa's dedicated practice hall.

KathakaliThrissur PooramArangettam VenueClassical MusicTemple Festivals
