⏰ Check-in & Check-out
What time is standard check-in?+

Standard check-in is 4:00 PM.

Unlike a hotel room, Dwarka Villa is a full 4-bedroom estate. Between 11 AM and 4 PM, our full crew works through every bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, and garden to prepare the entire property. We appreciate your understanding on this timing.

Arriving before 4 PM? You are welcome to park your vehicle in our compound and wait in a common area while we finish preparations.
Can I check in early?+

Yes — subject to availability and the following fees:

ScenarioConditionFee
1–2 hrs before 4 PMPrevious guests checked out on time25% of one night
Early morning / before noonPrevious night must be blocked50% of one night
Booked via Airbnb or OTA? Early check-in requires booking a full additional night through the platform. The discounted fee is only available for direct bookings.

Please request early check-in at least 48 hours in advance so we can plan accordingly.

Can I check in late at night or at odd hours?+

Yes — we accommodate late arrivals at any hour. Our caretaker lives nearby and will receive you.

  • Share your estimated arrival time in advance
  • For arrivals after midnight, WhatsApp us so we can ensure someone is ready
  • Your room will be fully prepared regardless of arrival time
We understand pilgrim travel involves overnight trains and early temple visits — we work around your schedule.
Can I check out late?+

Standard check-out is 11:00 AM. Late check-out follows the same structure as early check-in:

ScenarioConditionFee
Up to 2:00 PMNext guests not arriving same day25% of one night
Evening departure (after 4 PM)Next night must be blocked50% of one night
Booked via Airbnb? Late check-out requires booking a full additional night through the platform.

Please request late check-out at least 48 hours in advance.

Is there a minimum stay?+

We prefer a minimum of 2 nights given the time required to prepare the entire estate. Single-night stays are considered based on availability — please contact us directly.

For Guruvayur pilgrimage families, a 3–5 night stay is ideal to attend multiple darshans comfortably without rushing.

🍳 Meals & Housekeeping
Do you provide breakfast or meals?+

We do not provide meals as part of the standard stay. However:

  • The villa has a fully equipped modular kitchen — ideal for preparing your own traditional vegetarian or prasadam meals
  • We can connect you with a local cook specialising in Kerala vegetarian cuisine — fees negotiated directly with the cook
  • Several good vegetarian restaurants are within a 5-minute walk
Do you provide daily maid service?+

For standard stays (1–3 nights), we do not offer daily maid service — this is intentional.

Most guests travel with valuables — jewellery, cash, temple offerings. We prefer not to send outside staff into personal spaces and cannot accept liability for any unpleasant situations.

For extended stays, we can connect you with local help on request:

  • Cooking and general cleaning assistance
  • Baby carrying support
  • Charges negotiated directly with the helper
We recommend keeping bedroom cleaning personal. Fresh linens and a full clean at check-in are always provided.
🚗 Transport & Logistics
Do you provide airport or railway station pickup?+

Yes — we arrange pickups and drop-offs from:

  • Cochin Airport (COK) — 75 km, ~1.5 hr drive
  • Guruvayur Railway Station — 2 km, 5 min
  • Thrissur Railway Station — 30 km, ~45 min
  • Calicut Airport — 120 km, ~2.5 hrs

Transport is arranged through trusted local drivers. Please share your travel details at booking so we can organise in advance.

Can you hold luggage after check-out?+

Unfortunately, we cannot hold luggage or personal belongings after check-out.

As a private villa without a staffed reception desk, we cannot accept liability for stored items. Please carry all belongings upon departure.

If you need more time after check-out, consider a late check-out arrangement instead — see the check-out section above.
